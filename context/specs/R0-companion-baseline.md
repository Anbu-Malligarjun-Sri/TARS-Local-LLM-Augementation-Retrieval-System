# R0 companion baseline and bilingual desktop acceptance

The first audience is a prospective buyer trying a short synthetic conversation on their own phone or laptop. The desktop app remains the private operator reference. R1 will have a separate visitor service and build.

## Acceptance

- In one conversation, switch between English and Tamil, request an answer in the other language, and discuss a language by name. The current request controls the answer. Speech recognition has a separate input locale. Playback uses an installed local voice in the language of the generated sentence.
- Dry observations can accompany helpful Tamil or English answers within the existing audience and sensitivity policy. Keep urgent and respectful replies direct. Preserve code, names and quoted material.
- Provide an explicit visitor capability list, release decisions, asset inventory and a cost worksheet with units. Keep unapproved spend at zero. Record unknown business inputs as decisions for the owner.
- Exercise two synthetic scripts per language with local inference and record what was actually checked. Keep fixture data outside the owner store and exclude production credentials, transcripts and recordings. Distinguish text inference from acoustic tests.
- Capture reproducible startup evidence, update the tracker, and identify the first independently testable R1 service contract.
